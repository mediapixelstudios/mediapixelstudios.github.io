Questions

What do you need from us?

Your logo, brand colors and fonts, demo access or a screen recording of each feature, and one person who can approve each video within two business days.

Will it actually look like our product?

Yes. We rebuild the screens that matter from your real interface, then simplify them so the feature reads clearly on a phone. You approve the first video before we produce the rest.

Why not just record the screen?

Raw screen recordings are small, slow and full of noise on a phone. Rebuilt screens let us zoom, highlight and pace each step, and keep every video consistent with your brand.

What happens after the pilot?

If it's working for you, a monthly plan of 4, 8 or 12 videos keeps every release covered. If not, you keep the 4 videos and there's no commitment.

How do we pay?

Bank transfer or PayPal. Half to start, half when the four videos are approved.

Shipping something soon?

Tell us what's coming. We'll pick the four features that make the best videos.
